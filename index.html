About the Forum

Understanding Accreditation and Quality Assurance

Accreditation is a formal process used to evaluate whether an organization is competent to carry out specified conformity assessment activities. Depending on the scope, these activities can include testing, calibration, inspection, certification and other forms of evaluation.

Conformity assessment examines whether products, services, processes, systems or persons meet specified requirements. Clear requirements, competent personnel, impartial evaluation and reliable evidence are important to the credibility of assessment results.

This website presents general information about these fields and provides a place for IF-A's verified institutional publications and technical resources.

Important: Accreditation, certification and membership are different concepts. The scope and legal effect of any recognition depend on the applicable scheme, authority and documented arrangements.

Foundational Concepts

Principles That Support Confidence

These are widely relevant principles for credible assessment activities. Their implementation depends on the applicable organization, standard and assessment scheme.

Impartiality

Managing conflicts of interest and ensuring objective decisions.

Competence

Using appropriately qualified people and suitable methods.

Transparency

Making applicable requirements, processes and decisions clear.

Confidentiality

Handling protected information according to applicable obligations.

Accountability

Maintaining defined responsibilities and traceable records.

Consistency

Applying established criteria consistently within the defined scope.

Evidence

Supporting findings with relevant and reliable documented information.

Continual Improvement

Reviewing performance and addressing identified weaknesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Accreditation Explained

What is accreditation?

Accreditation is a formal attestation by an authorized accreditation body that a conformity assessment body is competent to perform specified activities within a defined scope.

How is accreditation different from certification?

Certification generally attests that a product, system, service, process or person meets specified requirements. Accreditation evaluates a conformity assessment body's competence for specified activities. The precise meaning depends on the applicable framework.

Does listing a standard mean an organization is accredited?

No. Referring to a standard or using its requirements does not, by itself, prove that an organization has been accredited. Accreditation status must be verified with the relevant accreditation body and within the applicable scope.

Does this website issue accreditation certificates?

This information portal does not itself issue certificates. Refer to IF-A's verified official policies and authorized channels for information about any actual services or schemes.

How can I verify a certificate or recognition claim?

Identify the issuing organization, certificate number, scope, validity and applicable accreditation body. Confirm the details directly through the relevant official register or issuer. Do not rely on a logo alone.

Where can I find official IF-A information?

Visit the main website at https://if-a.org/ and use its published contact details and official documents.
